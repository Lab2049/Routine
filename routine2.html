What changed in the routine
  • Time doesn’t fit at 3 h per chapter. The full list needs about 203 h; 17 days hold about 160 h. Gap chapters (★) get the most time. Other chapters get a one-pass 1.5 h (2 h for Math I(3), I(5), II(6); Chemistry set per chapter, 1 h for Physics I(1), I(10)).
  • One chapter at a time. A chapter stays active until it is finished, then the next chapter comes from the next subject (Physics → Chemistry → Math → Biology). Chapter order inside each subject is unchanged, and exam dates are not used for ordering. Chemistry gets one chapter per day (I(1), I(5), II(5) are read together on one day).
  • Midday dead time removed. Bath, prayer and lunch end by 2 PM instead of 4 PM. Nap is 30 min (4:50 PM), sleep stays at 12:30 AM (7.5 h).
  • DU QB block: kept on 16 Nov only. From 17 Nov that 1.5 h is revision time.
  • Exams: the six paper finals (18–28 Nov) are scheduled. Subject finals on 29 Nov – 2 Dec are optional and skipped by default; each one costs about half a revision day.
  • Spill-over: Dec 3 is included as an extra revision day and absorbs the chapters that do not fit by 2 Dec. Its optional subject final is skipped by default.
  • Added chapters: Chem II(4) (2 h), Phy I(1, 10), Math I(2), II(5), Bio I(2, 9, 12), II(7, 9) were missing from the sequence and are now included. Chem II(2) is excluded.
